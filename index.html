Q: How can I check whether a website is safe?

A: Check the complete domain carefully, look for HTTPS, review the privacy policy and terms, and avoid suspicious links. HTTPS alone does not guarantee that a website is legitimate.

Q: How can I find the correct website address?

A: Confirm the domain using independently verifiable sources. Use the official Exotic Animals Miami website for service information and avoid unverified websites requesting sensitive data.

Q: What should I do if I find a suspicious website?
